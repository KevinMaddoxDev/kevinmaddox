Organize Your Day, Your Way

  • 📅 Smart Views: Navigate your schedule effortlessly through a focused Today view, a comprehensive Week view, or an All Reminders master list.
  • ⏰ Advanced Notifications: Never miss a beat. Configure up to three distinct alerts (Pre, Main, and Post) for every single reminder.
  • 🔁 Flexible Recurrence: Easily set alarms to trigger once, daily, weekly, or at custom tailored intervals.
  • ⚙️ Granular Control: Customize each notification's sound, vibration, and duration. You can even set critical alarms to bypass 'Do Not Disturb' mode.
  • 🎨 Visual Sorting: Assign custom accent colors to your notifications to instantly identify routines at a glance.

Why Choose Daily Reminders?

Built to eliminate the stress of managing repeated alarms, Daily Reminders handles everything from simple subtle nudges to aggressive lock-screen alarms. Whether you need a gentle notification for a meeting or a persistent alarm for daily medication, you have total control over how, when, and how loudly you are reminded.

Upcoming Features

  • 🏷️ Tags & Filtering: Better organize and search through your growing list of habits using custom tags.
  • ⚡ Premade Configurations: Save and reuse your favorite notification setups instantly (e.g., 'Work Meeting' or 'Medicine').
  • 👆 Gesture Navigation: Swipe left and right seamlessly to jump between days and weeks.
  • 🧹 Auto-Cleanup: Automatically delete reminders after completion or after a specified time frame.
